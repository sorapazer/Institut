# English translation files

General rules
- Translate into precise, formal academic English (American spelling: "behavior", "center"). Keep the scholarly register of the German original.
- Institute name: "Institute for Meaning-Centered Psychology". Journal: "Journal of Meaning-Centered Psychology" (German title "Zeitschrift für Sinnzentrierte Psychologie", abbreviation ZSP stays).
- Established English terms for logotherapy (Frankl's own English terminology): will to meaning, freedom of will, meaning of life, existential vacuum, existential frustration, noogenic neurosis, noetic dimension, dimensional ontology, defiant power of the human spirit, self-distancing, self-transcendence, paradoxical intention, dereflection, hyperreflection, hyperintention, anticipatory anxiety, modification of attitudes, Socratic dialogue, creative / experiential / attitudinal values, tragic triad, tragic optimism, homo patiens, supra-meaning, conscience as the organ of meaning, Sunday neurosis, pandeterminism.
- Do NOT translate bibliographic references (keep literature entries as they are). Do translate titles of works only when mentioned in running text, adding the original in parentheses where helpful, e.g. *Ärztliche Seelsorge* (The Doctor and the Soul).
- Keep legal terms of the German system accurate and explain them: Heilpraktikergesetz (German Non-Medical Practitioners Act), Approbation (state license), Richtlinienverfahren (procedures reimbursed by statutory health insurance), § 203 StGB (German Criminal Code) etc. German hotline numbers stay unchanged.
- Never invent facts, studies or numbers that are not in the German original.
- Output valid JavaScript, strings in double quotes, inner quotes as “…” curly quotes or escaped.
- Every file starts with:  window.EN = window.EN || {};
